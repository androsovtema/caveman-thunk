Privacy Policy

Shorts Lab Analytics · Effective October 13, 2026

Shorts Lab Analytics ("the app") is an internal tool operated by the Caveman Thunk team. It is used only with the team's own YouTube channel. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the app uses these Google OAuth scopes:

  • youtube.readonly: the channel's basic information, its list of uploaded videos and their public statistics (views, likes, comments).
  • yt-analytics.readonly: YouTube Analytics reports for the channel's own videos, such as views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources and aggregated viewer geography.
  • youtube.upload: uploading the channel's own videos and thumbnails.

YouTube Analytics data is aggregated by YouTube. The app does not receive or request personal information about individual viewers.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload the channel's own videos and to measure how those videos perform, so the team can improve future episodes. The data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to build profiles of any person.

Storage and security

Reports are stored as files on the team's own computer. The OAuth refresh token is stored in the operating system's secure keychain and is never published. No data is stored on third-party servers by the app.

Sharing

The app does not share Google user data with third parties.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The app uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. After access is revoked, the app can no longer fetch data, and locally stored reports are deleted on request.

Contact

Changes
